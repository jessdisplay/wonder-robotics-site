The spices

Your blends stay yours.

Chef Mahesh grinds each blend and packs it by weight, one sealed kit for each batch, and the kettle calls for it at its step. The recipe never lives in the machine.

Heat by the shot

Mild, medium or hot is a measured shot of chilli paste, so a medium vindaloo is the same medium every night.

Wet, not dry

To order, spice goes in as paste and spiced ghee. Powders cake in a kitchen full of steam.

The tadka

Mustard seed, cumin and curry leaf bloom in ghee at the start of every batch, and again on each dal.

Marinades

Tikka, tandoori and Chicken 65 marinate overnight in the cold room. The machines only cook them.

Herbs

Coriander, mint and fresh curry leaf go on by hand at the pass.
