One order, start to finish

A butter chicken, from the morning pot to the table or the driver's bag.

  1. Stock pot boilerTadka in ghee, then the makhani base in a big pot, stirred, about two hours2 h
  2. Cold roomChicken breast in the tandoori marinade, overnight
  3. Combi ovenRoasted on trays, combi steam and dry heat, 18 minutes, then diced18 min
  4. Cold roomBase and protein weighed into one kit pot per dish, lidded, labelled
  5. Line fridgeHands out the Butter Chicken kit: makhani base and tandoori chicken20 s
  6. Finishing stationKit tipped into the drum. The station heats and stirs it, then adds butter and cream2.5 min
  7. Finishing stationA full rinse, because this gravy carries nuts45 s
  8. Dine-in: the passInto the serving bowl, finished with a swirl of cream25 s
  9. Delivery: the pack benchInto a 650 ml tub, lid pressed down, a swirl of cream in a side pot so it arrives fresh35 s

The spices

Your blends stay yours.

Chef Mahesh grinds each blend and packs it by weight, one sealed kit for each pot of base, labelled and dated. The recipe never lives in a machine.

Heat by the shot

Mild, medium or hot is a measured shot of chilli paste, so a medium vindaloo is the same medium every night.

Wet, not dry

To order, spice goes in as paste and spiced ghee. Powders cake in a kitchen full of steam.

The tadka

Mustard seed, cumin and curry leaf bloom in ghee at the start of every pot, and again over each dal.

Marinades

Tikka, tandoori and Chicken 65 marinate overnight in the cold room, then go to the line fridge in portions.

Herbs

Coriander, mint and fresh curry leaf go on by hand at the pass.
