The whole job

The machine is a third of it.

The counter, the room and the brand are the rest. In a new venue or the one you have, we take the job from the idea to the first service, and stop after any stage you like.

Design

The bar, the kiosk, the kitchen or the counter it sits in. Drawn to your floor plan: plan, general arrangement, the concept art, the colours.

Build and fit out

Joinery, stone, services, the skin, the signage, the screen. Our trades or yours, to our drawings.

Brand

The mark, the colours, the cups, the bags, the menu, the uniforms. One system across the machine and the room.

Engineering

The cell around the machine: guarding, services, extraction, the counter it lives in, the program it runs.

Software

Ordering, payment, the menu on the screen, the dashboard, the numbers.

Keep it running

Spares, monitoring, updates, a number that answers. Standard or priority, priced by the year on the quote.
