[ Questions ]
Is it real espresso?

Yes. Ground to order on an Eversys: one arm pulls the shot, the other steams the milk and pours.

What happens when it breaks?

The service covers repairs, parts and labour for the first 24 months. We aim to answer a critical outage within four business hours and be on site within two business days, within 100 km of the Valley.

Who looks after it day to day?

Your team, trained by us at commissioning. Someone restocks, cleans daily and holds the food licence. The robot replaces the barista, not the owner.

What is a balloon?

A lump sum left to the end of the term. It lowers the weekly repayment and is paid, or refinanced, when the term ends. Up to half the price, if the lender agrees.

How is it taxed?

A lease, a hire purchase and a chattel mortgage are taxed differently, and at {{coffee:price}} the bar is above the instant asset write-off limit. Your accountant should confirm before you sign.

What does it need on site?

About two square metres, power, water and drainage. You arrange the site, its approvals and the food business licence. We draw it, and our trades or yours put it in.

Are there smaller formats?

Yes. The B Standard bar and the Smart EFF vending format are priced in the quote. Price one.

Can I taste it first?

Yes. Ours pours at 365 St Pauls Terrace, Fortitude Valley. Come and order one before you order one: book a tasting or call 1800 983 404.
