A dumpling kitchen that runs itself, from the order to the pass, for a Queensland group with two dozen venues. It's being built now at the Gold Coast Health and Knowledge Precinct.

The picture below is a concept, not the kitchen. Photography follows the opening.

What's being built

The same system that runs our own kitchen in Fortitude Valley, scaled for a single-menu, high-volume dumpling operation: cooking, steaming and plating by machine, orders in from the precinct, food out through a pass. Details on the machines, the throughput and the opening date will be published here as the build lands.
