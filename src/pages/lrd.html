Thirteen dumpling shops in, Little Red Dumpling wanted the fourteenth to cook without a brigade behind it. A long narrow tenancy at Nexus Way, a raked roof four metres up, glazing the whole length of one wall.

The machines are on site. The images below are ours, made from the walkthrough of the room, showing what it looks like finished. Photography replaces them the week it opens.

What goes in

A six-axis arm on a linear rail down the cook line, working behind glass so the room can watch it. Six boiling wells for dumplings and noodles, bamboo steamers stacked at the pass, extraction the length of the counter. Orders land from the screens and the app; the arm plates into bowls and the bowls go out on the pass.

Everything under the counter is ours too: the ordering, the payment, the stock counts, the dashboard the manager watches on a phone. The same stack that runs our own kitchen in Fortitude Valley, set to a single-menu, high-volume dumpling operation.

The site today

This one is real. Gino walked the tenancy in September: stud framing up, services in the ceiling, laser levels running the length of the floor, and the cooking units already delivered and standing on the slab in their wrapping.

Throughput, opening date and the finished photography go here as the build lands. If you are planning something like it, the quickest way to understand it is to come and watch ours run in Fortitude Valley.
