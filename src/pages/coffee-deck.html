      Rates
      {{coffee:rates}} a year is the indicative range. The rate, fees, deposit and any balloon are set when the application is assessed.
      Ownership
      Title passes when every repayment and any balloon has been paid, as set out in the finance contract.
      Service
      Included for the first 24 months within the price. From month 25 it is optional at {{coffee:after}} a month and is not in the headline figures unless selected.
      Margin
      74% is an estimate of what stays after beans, milk, cups and syrups. Site costs, power, card fees and restocking time are counted separately.
      Operation
      The robot replaces the barista, not the owner. Someone restocks, cleans daily and holds the food business licence.
      Earnings
      Sales depend on the site, price and volume. The calculators show scenarios, not a promise.
      Tax
      A lease, a hire purchase and a chattel mortgage are taxed differently. The equipment is above the instant asset write-off limit. Your accountant should confirm.
      Site
      The buyer arranges the site, its approvals, power and water.
