The containerkitchen

Site
Eat Street Northshore, Hamilton, Brisbane
Format
Twenty foot shipping container, one side opening to a serving hatch
Machine
Six-axis arm on a rail, cooking line behind glass
Skin
Micro-LED wrap across three faces of the box
Scope
Planning, design, brand, fabrication, implementation, maintenance
Status
In development

Eat Street: 180 containers, seventy kitchens, Friday to Sunday. People stop to watch an arm cook.

The arm cooks behind glass. The skin is a screen.

The brief

Our own container for Eat Street Northshore: 180 containers, seventy kitchens, three nights a week. Win the three seconds someone gives a stall.

Planning

Signage does not win three seconds. An arm working behind glass does.

A container is 6 metres by 2.4. Put an arm in it and the window becomes the show.

Design

One side out for the hatch, which folds up as the awning. Arm on a rail, extraction through the roof, counter the length of the opening.

The skin is the brand pass. Micro-LED cladding on three faces. One brand on Friday, another on Saturday.

Supply

A twenty foot high-cube. Arm, rail, fryer, hot line, extraction, glass, hatch. LED panels and the controller. Our stack behind the counter.

Implementation

The box arrives as a box. Cut, reframe, line, insulate, stainless, arm. All in our yard, so it has cooked before it meets the public.

Maintenance

Trades three nights, sits in our yard four. The easiest service schedule we have.

The outcome

In development. The first trading weekend goes here.
