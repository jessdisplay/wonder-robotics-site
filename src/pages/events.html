Robotics andHardware Club

What
A club for the people building robots and hardware in Queensland. One night a month, in the building
Where
365 St Pauls Terrace, Fortitude Valley, in the building, with the machines running
When
Monthly, evenings. First date to be announced
Who
Engineers, founders, researchers, students, operators, anyone shipping hardware
Tickets
On Humanitix, once the first date is set

Once a month we open the building to the people who build things. Two short talks from engineers who have shipped real hardware, the kitchen, the bar and the fleet running while you're here, the workshop bench open for whatever you're working on, and Gino somewhere in the room, delighted to show you what's under the hood.

No panel, no pitch deck, no sponsor slot. Robots, food, and the people who make them.

A club night

The talks. Two, twenty minutes each, from people who have shipped something with motors in it. What they built, what broke, what they'd do differently. Questions after, at the bar.

The floor. The robot kitchen, the bar and the dessert kiosk run through the night. Walk in behind the glass, watch the arm work, ask how the line is programmed.

The bench. The workshop is open. Bring the board, the arm, the prototype, the thing that doesn't work yet. There are engineers in the room who have seen it before.

The food. Cooked by the machines you came to see. Drinks poured by the arm at the bar.

Speak, show, or bring people

If you've shipped hardware and want to talk about it, or you're building something and want to put it on the bench, write to us. If you run a lab, a course or a team, bring them; group tickets are on the Humanitix page once it's live.

The first date and the ticket link land here as soon as they're set. Leave your address with the button above and you'll hear first.
