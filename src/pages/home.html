How we work

Planning, design, supply, implementation, maintenance.

The same five stages whether it is one robot arm, a software platform or a whole fitout. You deal with the same team through all of them, and you can stop after any one of them.

  1. 01Planning

    What the job actually is, what a machine should do with it, what it costs and what it gives back. On your site or on our floor, before anyone commits.

  2. 02Design

    Layouts against your real floor plan, the cell and its safety, the software and data model, the brand if it needs one. Piloted on our own floor so the design is proven before we cut anything.

  3. 03Supply

    Machinery from any maker alongside the machines we build ourselves, freight and delivery, all on one quote. Plenty of jobs stop here: you wanted a robot, not a project.

  4. 04Implementation

    Fabricate, program, integrate, install, commission and train your staff on it. Piloted again on the real line before we hand it over, so your floor is never the test bed.

  5. 05Maintenance

    Service schedules, spares, remote monitoring, software updates and a number in Brisbane that answers. Priced on the same quote as the machine.
