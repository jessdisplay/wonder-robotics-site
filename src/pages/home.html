A kitchen that runs itself is only the start. We do the whole job around it: the scope, the design, the brand, the build and the running of it. One team, one building, so nothing falls between vendors.

    We walk your site, or you walk ours. What the kitchen should cook, how many covers, what the floor and the services can take.

  2. Design

    The kitchen laid out around your menu. The machines picked for the job, from any maker. Drawings a builder can price.

  3. Brand

    The concept, the name, the look on the counter, the screens and the boxes. It all comes from one building, so it all matches.

  4. Build

    We supply, install, program and commission. About two months from order to first service, with your people trained on it.

  5. Run

    Maintenance, spares, software updates and remote monitoring, with a Brisbane number that answers.
