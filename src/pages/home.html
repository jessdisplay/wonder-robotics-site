The process

One team takes it from the first conversation to the first service.

Most robotics projects fall apart between vendors: the designer, the importer, the installer, the software people. We are all of them, in one building, so nothing falls between.

Design and develop

We don't just install machines. We design them.

Behind the kitchen is a workshop. The dessert kiosk, the bar counter, the software that runs the line and the routes the floor robots follow were all designed and built here. When the machine you need doesn't exist yet, we develop it, and we test it on our own floor before it goes on yours.

The people

A robotics company that's actually fun to visit.

The building is a bit of a playground. Gino built most of what runs in it and is the happiest man in the room when something new moves for the first time. Pepper meets you at the door. Come and see.
