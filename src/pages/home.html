How we work

Planning, design, supply, implementation, maintenance.

The same five stages whether it is one robot arm, a software platform or a whole fitout. Same team through all of them. You can stop after any one.

  1. 01Planning

    What the job is, what a machine would do with it, what it costs and what it saves. On your site or on our floor, before anyone commits.

  2. 02Design

    Layouts against your real floor plan, the cell and its safety, the software and data model, the brand if it needs one. Piloted on our own floor so the design is proven before we cut anything.

  3. 03Supply

    Machinery from any maker, alongside what we build ourselves. Freight and delivery on the same quote. A lot of jobs stop here, and that is fine.

  4. 04Implementation

    Fabricate, program, integrate, install, commission, train your staff. We pilot it on the real line before we hand it over.

  5. 05Maintenance

    Service schedules, spares, remote monitoring, software updates. A Brisbane number that answers. Priced on the same quote as the machine.
