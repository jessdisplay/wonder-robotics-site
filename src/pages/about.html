[ The two of us ]

The reason we can do the whole thing is that the two halves of it sit in one building. Gino builds robots from the ground up, hardware and software. Jesse has spent twenty years in brand, most of it taken all the way to the factory floor. Concept, brand and machine get argued out in the same room, usually over lunch the kitchen cooked.

Gino Feng
Robotics, hardware and fitout
Founder and chief executive of Wonder Byte Technology. A decade in software before robotics. Leads the engineering team behind our own kitchen, works with AgiBot, Unitree and UBTech, and built the WonderBot platform.
Jesse Costelloe
Brand, fabrication and product
Twenty years in brand, most of it taken to the factory. Founder of Kin Ventures, a venture studio across healthcare and hospitality, backed through The University of Queensland via UQ Ventures and the UniQuest Extension Fund.
