The brief

Our own. Build the robot kitchen first, open it to the public, then sell it. A warehouse at 365 St Pauls Terrace, open six days.

Planning

Nobody buys a robot kitchen from a brochure. They buy it after it cooks their lunch. Everything we install elsewhere runs here first.

Design

Kitchen on the long wall, pass at the end so the room can watch. A curved bar. The kiosk where the queue forms. The floor mapped for CadeBots.

The brand pass is Wonder itself: the crescent, the cream, the night.

Supply

A cobot on a rail with an ingredient store, three fryers, six noodle baths. A bar cobot. A kiosk arm. Two CadeBots and a Cruzr. Our stack behind it.

Implementation

Orders from the screens. The arm picks, fries, boils, plates. The bar pours to order. The kiosk runs itself. CadeBots run pass to table.

Maintenance

We run it six days, so we are our own first service call. Once a month the club has the building.

The outcome

Open six days. Full menu, nobody at the pass. Photographed, not rendered.
