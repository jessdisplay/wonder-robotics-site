[ Questions ]
Agent or chatbot?

An agent works inside a process: retrieves context, follows rules, uses connected software, completes or hands over. A chatbot answers questions and stops.

Where do you start?

Internal workflows: approvals, service requests, support triage, sales coordination, back-office processing.

Prototype first?

Yes, a scoped pilot in one live workflow is the usual first step.

Customer-facing?

Yes, when the process is well-scoped, knowledge is current and a person can step in quickly.
